# Demo click path (about 90 seconds)

For Connor, to go with the 2-minute outline. A suggestion: change it to suit you. Everything is made-up data.

**Before you start:** open the board (`index.html`) and the tenant page (`tenant.html`) side by side, in the **same browser**. A private window gives a clean start.

1. **The board** (`index.html`): "Homes most at risk come first, and each one says why." Point at the top card's reasons. Mention the **cold snap** banner: a forecast moves up homes that are hard to heat with someone vulnerable.
2. **A tenant reports damp** (`tenant.html`): reference **H-104**, tick "There's damp or mould", choose how to get in touch, then **Send**. Point out the kind wording ("Damp and mould are not your fault").
3. **Back on the board:** H-104 is now at the top as **New report: check today**, with the **Awaab's Law clock** already started. The law starts the clock when the landlord hears, not when an officer gets round to it.
4. **The clock at work:** open **2/1, 44 Ferry View (H-117)**. It's been investigated, so the board has **drafted the letter to the tenant** for the 3-day legal step.
5. **A person decides:** tick an action (energy advice, a damp check), say how urgent, tick "I've checked the reasons", then **Book**.
6. **The big picture:** **Manager summary** (deadlines due and missed, risk by area), then **Map** (every home by risk; shape, not just colour).

**One line to finish:** "Built by a delivery manager and a QA engineer in an afternoon. Next we'd check our assumptions with Cloch, and test it with real tenants and officers."
