# Mould Breakers: 2-minute show and tell

Presenter: Connor Archibald. Slides: http://localhost:8765/show-and-tell.html (click, or press → to move on). Don't go full screen, so you can switch tabs for the live demo. About 2 minutes.

## Slide 1: Mould Breakers (0:00 to 0:10)

"We're Mould Breakers: Glory, a delivery manager, and me, a QA engineer. We took the healthier homes challenge: finding tenants at risk from damp and cold before they get ill."

## Slide 2: Who it's for (0:10 to 0:25)

"It's for Cloch's housing officers. Most damp and mould is only found when a tenant reports it, and the people most at risk are often the ones who never get in touch. Awaab's Law now gives landlords strict deadlines."

## Slide 3: Live demo (0:25 to 1:05)

Click **1. Open the tenant page**. Say: "A tenant tells us about damp. Notice the wording: 'Damp and mould are not your fault.'" Choose "I live there", type **H-104**, tick "There's damp or mould", choose "Phone call", then **Send**.

Come back to the slides tab and click **2. Open the officer board**. Say: "On the officer's board, homes most at risk come first and each says why. H-104 is now flagged 'New report: check today', and the Awaab's Law clock has already started, because the law starts it when the landlord hears."

Come back to the slides tab and press →.

## Slide 4: What we built (skip if short of time)

"Officers also get help reaching tenants who've gone quiet, monthly reminders, a manager summary, a map and cold snap alerts."

## Slide 5: Built by two people who aren't developers (1:05 to 1:20)

"Neither of us is a developer. We made about 50 shares with no clashes, there are 54 automated checks, and I tested every page with a screen reader."

## Slide 6: What didn't work (1:20 to 1:35)

"What didn't work: it's a convincing demo, not a service. The two sides only link up in the same browser. And Claude made mistakes too, but checking caught every one."

## Slide 7: The surprise (1:35 to 1:52)

"The surprise was how it pushed back like a careful colleague. It pointed out a neighbour can't agree to share a tenant's health details, wouldn't put made-up homes on real people's houses, and checked every helpline number."

## Slide 8: What's next (1:52 to 2:00)

"Next, we'd test it with real officers and tenants. Thank you."
