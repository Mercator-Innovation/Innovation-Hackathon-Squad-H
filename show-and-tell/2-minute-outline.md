# Mould Breakers: 2-minute show and tell

Presenter: Connor Archibald. Slides: http://localhost:8765/show-and-tell.html (click, or press → to move on). Stay out of full screen, so you can switch tabs for the demo.

## Slide 1: Mould Breakers (0:00 to 0:15)

"We're Mould Breakers: Glory, a delivery manager, and me, a QA engineer. We built a tool for Cloch's housing officers to find tenants at risk from damp and cold before they get ill, including the ones who never get in touch."

## Slide 2: Live demo (0:15 to 1:00)

Click **1. Open the tenant page**. Say: "A tenant tells us about damp. 'Damp and mould are not your fault.'" Choose "I live there", type **H-104**, tick "There's damp or mould", choose "Phone call", then **Send**.

Go back to the slides tab and click **2. Open the officer board**. Say: "Homes most at risk come first, and each says why. H-104 is now flagged, and the Awaab's Law clock has already started, because the law starts it when the landlord hears."

Go back to the slides tab and press →.

## Slide 3: What didn't work (1:00 to 1:25)

"What didn't work: it's a convincing demo, not a service. The two sides only link up in the same browser. And Claude made mistakes too, but checking caught every one."

## Slide 4: The surprise (1:25 to 2:00)

"The surprise was how it pushed back like a careful colleague. It pointed out a neighbour can't agree to share a tenant's health details, wouldn't put made-up homes on real people's houses, and checked every helpline number. And neither of us is a developer. Thank you!"
