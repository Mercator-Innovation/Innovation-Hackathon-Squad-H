# Mould Breakers: 2-minute show and tell

Presenter: Connor Archibald. Slides: http://localhost:8765/show-and-tell.html (click, or press → to move on; F for full screen). About 250 spoken words, roughly 15 seconds a slide.

## Slide 1: Mould Breakers (0:00 to 0:10)

"We're Mould Breakers: Glory, a delivery manager, and me, a QA engineer. We took the healthier homes challenge: finding tenants at risk from damp and cold before they get ill."

## Slide 2: Who it's for (0:10 to 0:30)

"It's for Cloch's housing officers. Most damp and mould is only found when a tenant reports it, and the people most at risk are often the ones who never get in touch. On top of that, Awaab's Law now gives landlords strict deadlines: investigate in 10 working days, tell the tenant in 3, start repairs in 5, finish in 20."

## Slide 3: What we built (0:30 to 1:00)

"So we built two sides. Officers get a board with the homes most at risk first, the reasons in plain words, and a person always decides. It runs the Awaab's Law deadlines, and it helps officers reach tenants who've gone quiet: a letter, then a call, then a door knock. Tenants get a kind, no-blame page, 'Damp and mould are not your fault', and their report reaches the officer straight away."

## Slide 4: Built by two people who aren't developers (1:00 to 1:15)

"Neither of us is a developer. We made about 50 shares with no clashes, there are 54 automated checks, and I tested every page with a screen reader."

## Slide 5: What didn't work (1:15 to 1:35)

"What didn't work: it's a convincing demo, not a service. The two sides only link up in the same browser. And Claude made mistakes too, like a phone layout bug and a miscounted test, but checking caught every one."

## Slide 6: The surprise (1:35 to 1:50)

"The surprise was how it pushed back like a careful colleague. It pointed out a neighbour can't agree to share a tenant's health details. It wouldn't put made-up homes on real people's houses. And it checked every helpline number before using it."

## Slide 7: What's next (1:50 to 2:00)

"Next, we'd test it with real officers and tenants. Thank you."
