# Solution summary: Challenge 1, Healthier homes

Drafted by Connor Archibald (merge captain) with Claude, 22 October 2026. **A draft for the squad to change.** Builds on [Glory's backlog](backlog-challenge-1.md) and [research note](research-awaabs-law-and-cloch.md). The product owner records the challenge and the idea in `TEAM.md`.

## Who it's for first

A Cloch **housing officer**, deciding which tenants to help first, before they get ill. The tenant check-in and the "quiet homes" view come later.

## What flags a home

Several signals together, never one alone:

- **Home condition:** low energy rating (EPC), older property, repeat condensation or leak jobs.
- **Money worries:** rent arrears, asking for fuel vouchers.
- **Gone quiet:** no access at the gas safety check, no contact for a long time.
- **Support notes:** from energy advice or tenancy visits.
- **Age:** older tenants get a regular check-in, and move up the list when other signals are present too.

## How it looks

- Homes listed **most at risk first**.
- Risk shown in **words plus colour** (for example "High: act this week"), never colour alone.
- Every flag shows its **reasons in plain words**, such as "3 condensation jobs in a year".

## What the officer can do

- Book **energy advice** (the home-visit energy advice service).
- Book a **damp check**. This starts the Awaab's Law clock.
- Arrange a **wellbeing call** from the community support team.
- **Send a letter or questionnaire** listing conditions for the tenant to tick and send back. A good way to reach tenants who never get in touch.

A person always makes the final call.

## Privacy

Health and support notes are only used **if the tenant has agreed** (consent).

## Next, straight after the first version

- The **Awaab's Law clock**: investigate in 10 working days, written summary in 3, start repairs in 5, finish in 20.
